Editing cards inside the deck

These are static mockups that open from disk. They use PipGlyph's colour tokens from app/globals.css, dark by default. Cards are grey placeholders; their "art" is the repo's own generated deck covers (public/defaults/deck-covers). The write-up is deck-in-place-editing-2026-10.md.

  1. Recommended (b): an edit drawer, 1366 × 768. Click a row, edit, then Save, Save & next, ‹ ›, or ✕ (which runs the unsaved-changes guard). "＋ Create new card" opens it too.
  2. Recommended (b) on a phone, 390 × 844. The same dialog fills the screen. Step through the four states.
  3. Bulk edit. Select, then Edit fields…, mixed values, Review (before → after), and Apply with Undo.
  4. Rejected (c): a section that opens in place, for comparison.

Option (a), a modal holding the full creator, is not drawn on its own. It is the drawer's below-1024 px form (mockup 2) used at every width; the doc explains why it is the fallback, not the pick.
